FILAMENT PHOTOS (optional)

Photos of your spools. Name them after the filament id in data/filamentos.js,
e.g. pla-preto.jpg, petg-transp.jpg, and set  foto: "images/filamentos/pla-preto.jpg"
on that filament.

If a filament has no photo, the site draws a spool in the filament's color instead.
Recommended: square, 400x400 px, JPG.
